# Product media

The [promotional capture](assets/product-preview.jpg) combines original product messaging with an actual browser capture of Cypher Heirs. [Dashboard capture](assets/dashboard-capture.jpg) is the unaltered application view used in the composition. The illustrated scan is an explicitly partial BTC/ETH/SOL selection; rankings and prices are a dated snapshot, not current trading guidance.

[Composition source](assets/promo.html) contains the layout. Serve the repository root over HTTP, open `docs/assets/promo.html`, and take a full-page capture of its fixed 1600 × 1000 canvas. It uses local CSS, system fonts and the real captured dashboard; no stock image, invented market result, remote font or tracking script is required.

To refresh it, scan the same three-symbol selection in the app, show the overview from the top, capture the app at its normal browser dimensions as `assets/dashboard-capture.jpg`, then capture the composition again as `assets/product-preview.jpg`. Update the visible snapshot date in the composition to match the capture.
